Off the clock

Kiwi in Melbourne. Self-proclaimed nerd.

I love checking countries off my bucket list (14 and counting!). The highlight so far was three months solo around Europe, which was the time of my life. Since crossing the ditch to Melbourne in 2026, I’ve been exploring closer to home. You’ll find me at the beach, or at a good pub trivia night.

I studied Creative Media Production at Massey University, specialising in video game development. That mix of creativity and problem solving drew me to front-end development and design, and I went on to study web development at Dev Academy Aotearoa. When I’m not building interfaces, I’m usually building a city in Cities: Skylines, a theme park in RollerCoaster Tycoon, a factory in Factorio, or an empire in Civilization.
